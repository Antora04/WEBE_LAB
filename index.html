ℹ️ About GameZone

🎮 What is GameZone?

GameZone is a free browser-based gaming portal featuring 16+ hand-crafted mini-games. Built for the CSE 3206 Web Engineering Lab, it showcases HTML5 Canvas, CSS3, and Vanilla JavaScript.

🔒 Privacy & Security

GameZone stores only your game scores and preferences in your browser's localStorage. No personal data is collected, transmitted, or shared. All games run locally — no external servers are used.

Note: Client-side validation demonstrated here does not replace server-side security in production applications.
